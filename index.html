About

Producer / DJ from the Pacific Northwest electronic scene

Schuz (pronounced “shoes”) grew up playing piano before music took a back seat through school. After college, a roommate who managed artists pulled him back in, and he started DJing around Seattle.

He began producing in 2016, trained at Point Blank Music School, and was selected for the Class of 808 program. His sound blends bass, trap and dance music, with releases on independent labels and features on EDM.com.

“I'm an avid believer in continuous training and finding new sounds that spark interest.”
