About

Producer / DJ from the Pacific Northwest electronic scene

Schuz (pronounced “shoes”) was raised learning piano, with music always around him. He stepped away from it through middle and high school to focus on academics and sports. After college, his roommate at the time was an artist manager, which pulled him back into the industry and onto the decks as a DJ around Seattle.

His journey as a producer started in May 2016 with his first music production class. He went on to train at Point Blank Music School and the Class of 808 program, and has since released a steady run of singles, the Synergy EP, label releases and collabs, with features on EDM.com.

“I'm an avid believer in continuous training and finding new sounds that spark interest.”
